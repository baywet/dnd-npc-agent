## Traits

### Pack Tactics
The kobold has advantage on an attack roll against a creature if at least one of the kobold's allies is within 5 feet of the creature and the ally isn't incapacitated.

### Legendary Resistance (3/Day)
If the Sovereign fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead. Its scales shimmer with prismatic light when it does.

### Voice of the Wyrm
The Sovereign speaks with the authority of a true dragon. Allied kobolds within 60 feet are immune to the charmed and frightened conditions while the Sovereign is conscious. When the Sovereign gives a verbal command to an allied kobold (no action required), that kobold has advantage on its next ability check or saving throw.

### Draconic Apotheosis
The Sovereign has undergone a partial draconic transformation. It has true draconic wings, its scales have hardened, and it radiates a palpable aura of draconic power. It counts as both a kobold and a dragon for the purposes of spells and effects.

### Magic Resistance
The Sovereign has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

## Actions

### Multiattack
The Wyrmspeaker Sovereign makes three attacks: two Wyrm Scepter attacks and one Sovereign's Decree.

### Wyrm Scepter (Magical)
*Melee or Ranged Spell Attack:* +9 to hit, reach 5 ft. or range 60 ft., one target.
*Hit:* 11 (2d6 + 4) bludgeoning damage (melee) or force damage (ranged) plus 10 (3d6) damage of the patron dragon's type.

### Sovereign's Decree
The Sovereign targets one creature it can see within 90 feet. The target must succeed on a DC 17 Wisdom saving throw or suffer one of the following effects (Sovereign's choice):

- **Kneel.** The target is knocked prone and its speed is reduced to 0 until the end of its next turn.
- **Flee.** The target must use its reaction to move up to its speed directly away from the Sovereign.
- **Turn.** The target must use its reaction to make one melee attack against a creature of the Sovereign's choice within its reach.

### Cataclysm Breath (Recharge 5–6)
The Sovereign channels the full fury of its dragon patron. It exhales energy in a 60-foot cone. Each creature in the area must make a DC 17 Dexterity saving throw, taking 45 (10d8) damage of the patron dragon's type on a failed save, or half on a success. Creatures that fail the save are also pushed 15 feet away from the Sovereign and knocked prone.

---

## Bonus Actions

### Sovereign's Will
The Sovereign commands up to four allied kobolds it can see within 60 feet. Each target can use its reaction to take the Attack, Dash, or Dodge action.

---

## Reactions

### Dragon's Rebuke
When the Sovereign takes damage from a creature within 60 feet, it can force that creature to make a DC 17 Constitution saving throw, taking 14 (4d6) damage of the patron dragon's type on a failure, or half on a success.

---

## Legendary Actions

The Sovereign can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action can be used at a time and only at the end of another creature's turn. The Sovereign regains spent legendary actions at the start of its turn.

### Reposition
The Sovereign flies up to half its speed without provoking opportunity attacks.

### Wyrm Bolt (Costs 2 Actions)
The Sovereign makes one ranged Wyrm Scepter attack against each creature of its choice within a 15-foot radius of a point it can see within 60 feet (maximum 3 targets).

### Draconic Barrier (Costs 2 Actions)
The Sovereign creates a wall of draconic energy along a line 30 feet long, 10 feet high, and 1 foot thick within 60 feet. A creature that enters the wall or starts its turn there takes 10 (3d6) damage of the patron dragon's type. The wall lasts until the start of the Sovereign's next turn.

### Awaken the Blood (Costs 3 Actions)
The Sovereign targets one allied kobold it can see within 30 feet. Until the end of the kobold's next turn, it grows temporary draconic features: its size becomes Medium, it gains 15 temporary hit points, its weapon attacks deal an extra 2d6 damage of the patron dragon's type, and it has advantage on all attack rolls.

---

## Lair Actions

*If encountered in the Dragon's Sanctum — the innermost chamber of a kobold mega-warren, decorated with dragon iconography and infused with draconic magic:*

On initiative count 20 (losing initiative ties), the Sovereign can take one lair action. It can't use the same effect two rounds in a row:

- **Tremor.** The ground shakes in a 30-foot radius around a point the Sovereign can see within 120 feet. Each creature in the area must succeed on a DC 15 Dexterity saving throw or be knocked prone. The area becomes difficult terrain until initiative count 20 of the next round.
- **Dragon's Eye.** Glowing draconic runes on the walls flare to life. Until initiative count 20 of the next round, the Sovereign and all allied kobolds in the lair have truesight out to 30 feet and cannot be surprised.
- **Reinforcements from Below.** 1d4 Kobold Tunnelers emerge from hidden warrens at points the Sovereign chooses within the lair. They act on initiative count 10.
- **Breath Vents.** Vents in the floor release a 20-foot-radius cloud of the patron dragon's energy type at a point within 60 feet. Each creature in the area must succeed on a DC 15 Constitution saving throw or take 10 (3d6) damage of that type and be poisoned until the end of its next turn.

## Tactics & Roleplaying Notes

**In Combat:** The Sovereign is a battlefield controller and force multiplier. It opens with Cataclysm Breath to devastate clustered enemies, then uses Sovereign's Decree to neutralize the most dangerous threats — forcing fighters to flee and spellcasters to kneel. It commands its minions with Sovereign's Will, sending waves of kobolds to exploit Pack Tactics on prone or isolated targets. It stays airborne whenever possible, using Reposition to maintain range. It saves Awaken the Blood for when a key minion can deliver a devastating blow on a vulnerable target. In its lair, it uses Tremor and Breath Vents to control space while reinforcements flood in.

**Personality:** The Sovereign has transcended ordinary kobold psychology. It speaks with measured authority in perfect Common and Draconic, its voice resonating with a bass harmonic that seems impossible for its frame. It does not grovel — not even before dragons, whom it treats as revered equals rather than superiors. This quiet confidence unnerves other kobolds almost as much as it inspires them. It is a philosopher-king who believes kobolds are the true inheritors of draconic legacy, and it is building a civilization to prove it.

**Lore:** The Wyrmspeaker Sovereign is a once-in-a-generation figure — a kobold whose dormant draconic bloodline has been fully awakened through decades of ritual, study, and communion with ancient dragon spirits. It has physically transformed: true wings, hardened scales, blindsight, and the ability to channel breath weapons. It commands not just a warren but a network of interconnected kobold colonies, and it has begun diplomatic outreach to chromatic dragons, offering loyalty in exchange for territory and legitimacy. Rumors suggest it seeks a dragon egg to raise as both a ward and an ultimate weapon.

**Encounter Suggestions:**
- The Sovereign commands a full kobold army besieging a dwarven stronghold — the party must infiltrate the warren to cut off the head (Deadly for level 10–12)
- The Sovereign offers a devil's bargain: help it recover a stolen dragon egg from a rival faction, and it will withdraw its forces from the region
- The party discovers the Sovereign is negotiating an alliance with an adult dragon — stopping the pact could prevent a regional catastrophe
- A rival kobold faction begs the party to help overthrow the Sovereign, whose "apotheosis" they consider heretical blasphemy against true dragons
