# Kobold Dragon Herald

*Small humanoid (kobold), lawful evil*

---

**Armor Class** 16 (dragon-scale vestments)
**Hit Points** 91 (14d6 + 42)
**Speed** 30 ft., fly 30 ft. (gifted wings, fragile)

|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|

**Saving Throws** Con +6, Int +7, Wis +5
**Skills** Arcana +7, History +7, Perception +5, Religion +7
**Damage Resistances** fire (or the damage type matching its patron dragon's breath weapon)
**Senses** darkvision 120 ft., blindsight 10 ft., passive Perception 15
**Languages** Common, Draconic, Infernal, Sylvan
**Challenge** 7 (2,900 XP)
**Proficiency Bonus** +3

---

## Traits

### Pack Tactics
The kobold has advantage on an attack roll against a creature if at least one of the kobold's allies is within 5 feet of the creature and the ally isn't incapacitated.

### Sunlight Sensitivity
While in sunlight, the kobold has disadvantage on attack rolls and on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on sight.

### Dragon's Chosen
The Herald has been blessed by a true dragon patron. It is immune to the frightful presence of dragons. When the Herald casts a spell that deals the damage type of its patron dragon's breath weapon, it deals an extra 1d6 damage of that type.

### Fragile Wings
The Herald has vestigial wings enhanced by draconic magic. If the Herald takes 15 or more damage in a single turn while flying, it must succeed on a DC 15 Constitution saving throw or lose its flying speed until the end of its next turn.

### Draconic Conduit
When the Herald is reduced to 0 hit points, it explodes in a burst of draconic energy. Each creature within 10 feet must make a DC 15 Dexterity saving throw, taking 14 (4d6) damage of the patron dragon's type on a failure, or half on a success.

---

## Actions

### Multiattack
The Dragon Herald makes two Draconic Bolt attacks, or one Draconic Bolt and one Breath Siphon attack.

### Draconic Bolt
*Ranged Spell Attack:* +7 to hit, range 120 ft., one target.
*Hit:* 13 (2d8 + 4) damage of the patron dragon's type.

### Wyrm Staff
*Melee Weapon Attack:* +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target.
*Hit:* 6 (1d6 + 3) bludgeoning damage plus 7 (2d6) damage of the patron dragon's type.

### Breath Siphon (Recharge 5–6)
The Herald channels a fragment of its patron's breath weapon in a 30-foot cone. Each creature in the area must make a DC 15 Dexterity saving throw, taking 28 (8d6) damage of the patron dragon's type on a failure, or half on a success.

---

## Spellcasting

The Herald is a 7th-level spellcaster. Its spellcasting ability is Intelligence (spell save DC 15, +7 to hit with spell attacks). It has the following spells prepared:

**Cantrips (at will):** *fire bolt, mending, prestidigitation*
**1st level (4 slots):** *absorb elements, shield, chromatic orb*
**2nd level (3 slots):** *dragon's breath, scorching ray*
**3rd level (3 slots):** *fireball, fly*
**4th level (1 slot):** *wall of fire*

*(Spell selections assume a red/fire dragon patron. Adjust to match other dragon types as needed.)*

---

## Bonus Actions

### Draconic Command
The Herald targets one allied kobold it can see within 30 feet. The target can use its reaction to move up to half its speed and make one weapon attack with advantage.

---

## Legendary Resistance (1/Day)
If the Herald fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead. Its scales flare with draconic light when it does so.

## Tactics & Roleplaying Notes

**In Combat:** The Dragon Herald stays at range, opening with Breath Siphon and following up with Draconic Bolt or fireball against clusters. It uses *shield* to survive incoming attacks and *fly* to maintain altitude advantage. It commands Tunneler minions with Draconic Command, sending them to flank or intercept melee threats. If cornered, it casts *wall of fire* to split the battlefield. Its Draconic Conduit makes it dangerous to kill in melee.

**Personality:** Imperious and deeply devout. The Herald considers itself the literal voice of dragonkind among kobolds and speaks with a gravity that belies its small stature. It refers to non-kobolds as "unscaled" and treats goblins with open contempt. Despite its arrogance, it is genuinely intelligent and a skilled negotiator — provided you acknowledge dragon superiority first.

**Lore:** Dragon Heralds are rare kobolds born with faint draconic lineage that has been awakened through ritual and patron blessing. They serve as high priests, advisors, and generals within kobold warrens, second in authority only to the dragon itself. A warren with a Herald is organized, disciplined, and far more dangerous than the norm.

**Encounter Suggestions:**
- The Herald leads a kobold ritual to awaken a dragon egg — the party must disrupt it before the ceremony completes
- 1 Herald with 1 Trapmaster and 8 Tunnelers defends a dragon's treasure vault (Hard for level 6–7)
- The Herald approaches the party as a diplomat, offering alliance in exchange for a service to its dragon patron
