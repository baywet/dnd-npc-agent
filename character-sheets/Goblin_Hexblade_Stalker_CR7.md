# Goblin Hexblade Stalker

*Small humanoid (goblinoid), lawful evil*

---

**Armor Class** 17 (shadow-woven leather, Charisma modifier)
**Hit Points** 97 (15d6 + 45)
**Speed** 30 ft., climb 20 ft.

|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|

**Saving Throws** Dex +7, Wis +4, Cha +7
**Skills** Arcana +5, Deception +7, Perception +4, Stealth +10
**Damage Resistances** necrotic
**Senses** darkvision 120 ft., passive Perception 14
**Languages** Common, Goblin, Sylvan
**Challenge** 7 (2,900 XP)
**Proficiency Bonus** +3

---

## Traits

### Nimble Escape
The goblin can take the Disengage or Hide action as a bonus action on each of its turns.

### Shadow Patron's Gift
The stalker's weapon attacks are magical and deal an extra 2d6 necrotic damage (included in attacks). While in dim light or darkness, the stalker has advantage on Stealth checks and can attempt to hide even when only lightly obscured.

### Hexblade's Curse (Recharges after a Short or Long Rest)
As a bonus action, the stalker curses one creature it can see within 30 feet for 1 minute. The stalker gains the following benefits against the cursed target:
- +3 bonus to damage rolls against the target
- Critical hits on rolls of 19–20 against the target
- If the cursed target dies, the stalker regains 15 hit points

### Evasion
When the stalker is subjected to an effect that allows a Dexterity saving throw for half damage, it instead takes no damage on a success and half damage on a failure.

---

## Actions

### Multiattack
The Hexblade Stalker makes three attacks: two with its Pact Blade and one with Eldritch Blast.

### Pact Blade (Shadow Glaive Form)
*Melee Weapon Attack:* +7 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target.
*Hit:* 8 (1d8 + 4) slashing damage plus 7 (2d6) necrotic damage.

### Eldritch Blast
*Ranged Spell Attack:* +7 to hit, range 120 ft., one target.
*Hit:* 9 (1d10 + 4) force damage, and the target is pushed 10 feet away.

### Shadow Step (Recharge 5–6)
The stalker teleports up to 60 feet to an unoccupied space it can see that is in dim light or darkness. After teleporting, it has advantage on the first melee attack it makes before the end of its turn.

---

## Spellcasting

The stalker is a 7th-level spellcaster. Its spellcasting ability is Charisma (spell save DC 15, +7 to hit with spell attacks). It has the following warlock spells:

**Cantrips (at will):** *minor illusion, prestidigitation*
**1st–4th level (2 4th-level slots):** *darkness, fear, shadow of moil, wrathful smite*

---

## Reactions

### Spectral Riposte
When a creature within 10 feet misses the stalker with a melee attack, the stalker can make one Pact Blade attack against that creature.

---

## Legendary Resistance (1/Day)
If the stalker fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead. When it does so, its shadow visibly flickers and distorts.

---

## Tactics & Roleplaying Notes

**In Combat:** The Hexblade Stalker opens by casting *darkness* centered on itself (it can see through it via its patron's gift), then applies Hexblade's Curse to the highest-value target. It uses Shadow Step to close distance, delivers Pact Blade strikes with necrotic damage, and retreats into darkness using Nimble Escape. It saves *fear* for when multiple enemies cluster together, and uses Spectral Riposte to punish melee attackers.

**Personality:** Cold, patient, and eerily polite. The stalker speaks in whispers and refers to its patron as "the Voice in the Dark." It views combat as a sacred ritual and always offers its victims a chance to surrender — not out of mercy, but because its patron feeds on despair. It collects small bones from its kills and carves runes into them.

**Lore:** Once a common goblin runt, the stalker was chosen by a shadow entity dwelling in the Feydark. It now serves as an assassin and envoy, moving between goblin tribes to enforce its patron's will. Other goblins fear and revere it.

**Encounter Suggestions:**
- The stalker hunts the party through a darkened ruin, engaging in hit-and-run tactics over multiple rounds
- It arrives as an emissary to negotiate on behalf of a powerful fey entity, turning hostile only if provoked
- The party must protect an NPC who has been marked by the stalker's Hexblade Curse
