*Small humanoid (goblinoid), lawful evil*

**Hit Points** 170 (20d6 + 100)
**Speed** 30 ft.

## Traits

### Nimble Escape
The goblin can take the Disengage or Hide action as a bonus action on each of its turns.

### Legendary Resistance (3/Day)
If the Warlord fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.

### Warlord's Presence
Allied creatures within 30 feet of the Warlord that can see or hear it have advantage on saving throws against being charmed or frightened and deal an extra 1d4 damage with weapon attacks.

### Crowned in Conquest
The Warlord has claimed dominion over multiple goblin clans through cunning and force. It wears a crown forged from the broken weapons of rival chieftains. While wearing the crown, the Warlord has truesight out to 30 feet and cannot be surprised.

### Uncanny Dodge
When an attacker that the Warlord can see hits it with an attack, it can use its reaction to halve the attack's damage.

---

## Actions

### Multiattack
The Warlord makes three attacks: two with Conqueror's Fang and one with Crown's Command.

### Conqueror's Fang (Magical Shortsword)
*Melee Weapon Attack:* +9 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target.
*Hit:* 12 (2d6 + 5) piercing damage plus 7 (2d6) psychic damage. On a critical hit, the target must succeed on a DC 16 Wisdom saving throw or be frightened of the Warlord until the end of its next turn.

### Crown's Command
The Warlord targets one creature it can see within 60 feet. The target must succeed on a DC 16 Charisma saving throw or be compelled to use its reaction to move up to half its speed in a direction of the Warlord's choice. This movement provokes opportunity attacks.

### Warcry of the Ascendant (Recharge 5–6)
The Warlord unleashes a devastating battle cry. Each hostile creature within 30 feet must make a DC 16 Constitution saving throw, taking 28 (8d6) thunder damage and being deafened for 1 minute on a failure, or half damage and no deafness on a success. Each allied goblinoid within 30 feet regains 10 hit points and can immediately move up to half its speed without provoking opportunity attacks.

---

## Bonus Actions

### Deploy the Warband
The Warlord commands up to three allied goblinoids it can see within 60 feet. Each target can use its reaction to make one weapon attack or move up to its speed.

---

## Reactions

### Uncanny Dodge
*(See Traits)*

### Retributive Strike
When the Warlord is hit by a melee attack, it can command one allied goblinoid within 5 feet of the attacker to use its reaction to make a melee attack against the attacker.

---

## Legendary Actions

The Warlord can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action can be used at a time and only at the end of another creature's turn. The Warlord regains spent legendary actions at the start of its turn.

### Move
The Warlord moves up to half its speed without provoking opportunity attacks.

### Strike (Costs 2 Actions)
The Warlord makes one Conqueror's Fang attack.

### Tyrant's Gaze (Costs 2 Actions)
The Warlord fixes its gaze on one creature within 30 feet. The target must succeed on a DC 16 Wisdom saving throw or be stunned until the end of its next turn. A creature that succeeds is immune to Tyrant's Gaze for 24 hours.

### Rally the Horde (Costs 3 Actions)
Each allied goblinoid within 60 feet that has 0 hit points but has not yet failed three death saving throws is stabilized and regains 1 hit point.

---

## Lair Actions

*If encountered in its war-camp or throne cavern, the Warlord can use lair actions.*

On initiative count 20 (losing initiative ties), the Warlord can take one lair action to cause one of the following effects. It can't use the same effect two rounds in a row:

- **Trap Sprung.** A 10-foot-square area within 60 feet becomes a pit trap. Each creature in the area must succeed on a DC 15 Dexterity saving throw or fall 10 feet, taking 3 (1d6) bludgeoning damage and landing prone.
- **Goblin Reinforcements.** 1d4 Goblin Skirmishers emerge from hidden tunnels at points the Warlord chooses within 60 feet. They act on initiative count 15.
- **Choking Smoke.** The Warlord ignites a smoke pot. A 20-foot-radius sphere of heavy smoke fills an area within 60 feet. The area is heavily obscured until initiative count 20 of the next round.

## Tactics & Roleplaying Notes

**In Combat:** The Warlord is a force multiplier. It opens with Warcry of the Ascendant to damage clustered enemies and heal allies, then uses Deploy the Warband to coordinate devastating focus fire. It stays behind its frontline, using Crown's Command to drag isolated enemies into its troops. It saves Legendary Resistance for crowd-control effects and uses Tyrant's Gaze to neutralize the party's most dangerous combatant. In its lair, it cycles through traps, reinforcements, and smoke to control the battlefield.

**Personality:** The Warlord Ascendant is terrifyingly competent — a goblin who has transcended the limitations of its kind through sheer force of will, ruthless intelligence, and a gift for reading enemies. It speaks all its languages fluently and without accent. It treats combat as a chess match and parley as a weapon. It is utterly pragmatic: it will ally with the party against a greater threat, betray them when convenient, and respect them if they prove strong enough to warrant it.

**Lore:** Once a nameless runt in a minor clan, the Ascendant united the fractured goblin tribes of the region through a combination of assassination, strategic alliances, and one legendary single combat against a bugbear warchief. It now commands hundreds of goblinoids and has begun forging pacts with hobgoblin legions and drow merchants. Kingdoms that once ignored the goblin warrens now send diplomats.

**Encounter Suggestions:**
- The Warlord commands a siege on a frontier town; the party must break the siege or negotiate terms (Hard–Deadly for level 10–12)
- The party is captured and brought before the Warlord's throne for judgment — escape, diplomacy, or trial by combat
- A rival faction hires the party to assassinate the Warlord before it unites the goblin nations
- The Warlord seeks an alliance with the party against a mutual enemy, offering goblin intelligence networks and tunnel access in exchange
