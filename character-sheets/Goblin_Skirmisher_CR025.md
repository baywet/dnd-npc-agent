## Traits

### Nimble Escape
The goblin can take the Disengage or Hide action as a bonus action on each of its turns.

### Pack Rat
The goblin has advantage on Wisdom (Survival) checks made to forage or scavenge for supplies.

---

## Actions

### Rusty Scimitar
*Melee Weapon Attack:* +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target.
*Hit:* 5 (1d6 + 2) slashing damage.

### Shortbow
*Ranged Weapon Attack:* +4 to hit, range 80/320 ft., one target.
*Hit:* 5 (1d6 + 2) piercing damage.

### Mud Toss (Recharge 6)
The goblin hurls a fistful of muck at a creature within 15 feet. The target must succeed on a DC 10 Dexterity saving throw or be blinded until the end of its next turn.

---

## Tactics & Roleplaying Notes

**In Combat:** Goblin Skirmishers fight dirty and flee quickly. They prefer to attack from hiding, using shortbows from behind cover before closing in with scimitars only when they outnumber foes. They use Nimble Escape to disengage the moment they take damage, retreating to re-hide and strike again.

**Personality:** Cowardly alone, emboldened in groups. Skirmishers chatter constantly in Goblin, taunting enemies and bickering with allies. They hoard small shiny objects and argue over loot mid-combat.

**Encounter Suggestions:**
- 4–6 Skirmishers ambush the party along a forest trail (Easy–Medium for level 1)
- 2 Skirmishers serve as scouts for a larger goblin war party
- A lone Skirmisher caught raiding a farmstead offers information in exchange for its life
