# Kobold Trapmaster

*Small humanoid (kobold), lawful evil*

---

**Armor Class** 15 (studded leather, trap-rigged shield)
**Hit Points** 49 (9d6 + 18)
**Speed** 30 ft., burrow 10 ft.

|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|

**Saving Throws** Dex +5, Int +5
**Skills** Investigation +5, Perception +3, Stealth +5, Thieves' Tools +7
**Senses** darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 13
**Languages** Common, Draconic, Gnomish
**Challenge** 3 (700 XP)
**Proficiency Bonus** +2

## Traits

### Pack Tactics
The kobold has advantage on an attack roll against a creature if at least one of the kobold's allies is within 5 feet of the creature and the ally isn't incapacitated.

### Sunlight Sensitivity
While in sunlight, the kobold has disadvantage on attack rolls and on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on sight.

### Master Engineer
The Trapmaster can use a bonus action to arm or disarm a trap within 5 feet. It has advantage on Intelligence (Investigation) checks to detect traps and on ability checks made to disarm them.

### Trap Sense
The Trapmaster and allied kobolds within 30 feet have advantage on saving throws against traps the Trapmaster has placed.

---

## Actions

### Multiattack
The Trapmaster makes two attacks with its Pick-Axe Blade.

### Pick-Axe Blade
*Melee Weapon Attack:* +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target.
*Hit:* 6 (1d6 + 3) piercing damage.

### Alchemist's Fire Flask
*Ranged Weapon Attack:* +5 to hit, range 20/60 ft., one target.
*Hit:* 7 (2d6) fire damage, and the target catches fire, taking 3 (1d6) fire damage at the start of each of its turns until a creature uses an action to extinguish the flames.

### Deploy Trap (3/Day)
The Trapmaster deploys one of the following traps in an unoccupied 5-foot square within 10 feet. Traps are hidden (DC 15 Investigation to detect) and trigger when a creature enters the space:

- **Spike Snare.** The triggering creature must succeed on a DC 13 Dexterity saving throw or take 7 (2d6) piercing damage and have its speed reduced to 0 until the end of its next turn.
- **Flashpowder Charge.** Each creature within 10 feet must succeed on a DC 13 Constitution saving throw or be blinded until the end of its next turn.
- **Glue Bomb.** The triggering creature must succeed on a DC 13 Strength saving throw or be restrained. A restrained creature can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, freeing itself on a success.

---

## Reactions

### Trigger Happy
When a creature the Trapmaster can see moves within 5 feet of one of its deployed traps, the Trapmaster can use its reaction to trigger the trap immediately, even if the creature hasn't entered the trap's space.

---

## Tactics & Roleplaying Notes

**In Combat:** The Trapmaster never engages without preparation. Ideally, its warren is pre-loaded with traps before intruders arrive. It opens by lobbing Alchemist's Fire at clustered enemies, then falls back through pre-mapped safe corridors, deploying traps behind it. It uses Trigger Happy to catch pursuers off-guard and lets its Tunneler minions swarm anyone caught in snares or glue.

**Personality:** Intensely proud of its work. The Trapmaster names each trap design and reacts with genuine hurt if enemies bypass one without triggering it. It speaks slowly and precisely in Common (unusual for a kobold), having learned the language from stolen engineering manuals. It has zero interest in melee combat and considers direct fighting "embarrassingly primitive."

**Encounter Suggestions:**
- 1 Trapmaster with 6–8 Kobold Tunnelers in a trapped mine complex (Medium for level 3–4)
- The Trapmaster has rigged a bridge or mountain pass with traps — the party must either disarm or negotiate passage
- A local town hires the party to deal with a Trapmaster whose pit traps have been catching livestock and travelers
