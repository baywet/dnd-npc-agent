## Traits

### Rampage
When the gnoll reduces a creature to 0 hit points with a melee attack on its turn, the gnoll can take a bonus action to move up to half its speed and make a bite attack.

### Abyssal Fury
The Fang of Ruin's weapon attacks are magical. When the Fang hits with a melee weapon attack, the attack deals an extra 2d6 necrotic damage (included in attacks). If the Fang has fewer than half its hit points, it instead deals an extra 3d6 necrotic damage.

### Relentless Hunger (Recharges after a Short or Long Rest)
If the Fang takes damage that would reduce it to 0 hit points, it is instead reduced to 1 hit point. Its eyes blaze with abyssal fire, and it has advantage on all attack rolls until the end of its next turn.

### Aura of Butchery
Each hostile creature that starts its turn within 10 feet of the Fang must succeed on a DC 13 Wisdom saving throw or be frightened until the start of its next turn. A creature that succeeds is immune to this aura for 24 hours.

---

## Actions

### Multiattack
The Fang of Ruin makes three attacks: two with its Ruinous Glaive and one with its Bite.

### Ruinous Glaive (Magical)
*Melee Weapon Attack:* +8 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target.
*Hit:* 10 (1d10 + 5) slashing damage plus 7 (2d6) necrotic damage (or 10 [3d6] necrotic if below half hit points).

### Bite
*Melee Weapon Attack:* +8 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target.
*Hit:* 7 (1d4 + 5) piercing damage plus 7 (2d6) necrotic damage. If the target is a creature, the Fang regains hit points equal to the necrotic damage dealt.

### Feast of Carnage (Recharge 5–6)
The Fang enters a whirlwind of violence, making one Ruinous Glaive attack against each creature of its choice within 10 feet. Each creature hit must also succeed on a DC 16 Strength saving throw or be knocked prone.

---

## Bonus Actions

### Demonic Surge
The Fang channels abyssal energy to take the Dash action. Until the end of this turn, opportunity attacks against the Fang are made with disadvantage.

---

## Reactions

### Savage Retaliation
When a creature within 10 feet deals damage to the Fang, the Fang can make one Bite attack against that creature.

---

## Legendary Resistance (1/Day)
If the Fang fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead. Abyssal ichor drips from its maw when it does.

## Tactics & Roleplaying Notes

**In Combat:** The Fang of Ruin is a berserker that gets more dangerous as it takes damage. It uses Demonic Surge to close distance rapidly, then wades into the thickest group of enemies, relying on Aura of Butchery to frighten weaker foes and Feast of Carnage to cleave through clusters. Its Bite attack provides sustain, letting it recover health mid-fight. Abyssal Fury means its damage spikes when bloodied — enemies should be wary of leaving it alive at low health. It fights to the death without exception.

**Personality:** The Fang of Ruin is not truly sapient in the way most NPCs are. It was once an ordinary gnoll that underwent a demonic awakening — a ritual exposure to raw Abyssal energy that burned away fear, mercy, and most higher cognition. It speaks in Abyssal in short, prophetic fragments ("The Jaw opens," "All meat," "He hungers"). It does not negotiate, threaten, or posture. It simply kills, eats, and moves on.

**Lore:** Fangs of Ruin are created, not born. When a gnoll warband's slaughter reaches a critical threshold, the Abyss takes notice. Yeenoghu's power infuses the most savage warrior, transforming it into a Fang — a living weapon of entropy. Other gnolls follow a Fang with ecstatic reverence, interpreting its garbled Abyssal utterances as divine commands. Where a Fang walks, civilization dies.

**Encounter Suggestions:**
- The Fang leads a raiding force of 1 Packmaster, 6 Scavengers, and 4 hyenas tearing through a village — the party arrives mid-assault (Hard for level 6–7)
- The Fang has been stalking the party for days, picking off NPCs in their caravan one by one, attacking only at night
- A desperate gnoll (one of the few that retained some sapience) begs the party to destroy the Fang before it leads the entire warband into a suicidal assault on a fortified city
