# Kobold Tunneler

*Small humanoid (kobold), lawful evil*

---

**Armor Class** 12 (natural armor)
**Hit Points** 7 (3d6 − 3)
**Speed** 30 ft., burrow 10 ft.

|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|

**Skills** Perception +2, Stealth +4
**Senses** darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 12
**Languages** Common, Draconic
**Challenge** 1/4 (50 XP)
**Proficiency Bonus** +2

---

## Traits

### Pack Tactics
The kobold has advantage on an attack roll against a creature if at least one of the kobold's allies is within 5 feet of the creature and the ally isn't incapacitated.

### Sunlight Sensitivity
While in sunlight, the kobold has disadvantage on attack rolls and on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on sight.

### Tunnel Rat
The kobold can move through spaces as narrow as 1 foot wide without squeezing. It has advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks made while underground.

---

## Actions

### Shiv
*Melee Weapon Attack:* +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target.
*Hit:* 4 (1d4 + 2) piercing damage.

### Sling
*Ranged Weapon Attack:* +4 to hit, range 30/120 ft., one target.
*Hit:* 4 (1d4 + 2) bludgeoning damage.

### Set Caltrop Line
The kobold scatters caltrops in a 5-foot square within 5 feet. Any creature that enters the area must succeed on a DC 12 Dexterity saving throw or stop moving and take 1 piercing damage. Until the creature regains at least 1 hit point, its walking speed is reduced by 10 feet.

---

## Tactics & Roleplaying Notes

**In Combat:** Tunnelers never fight alone. They cluster in groups of four or more to exploit Pack Tactics, swarming a single target with shivs while others sling stones from cover. They scatter caltrops in chokepoints and retreat through narrow tunnels the party can't follow. If reduced to half strength, they flee deeper into their warren.

**Personality:** Nervous, skittish, and fanatically loyal to whoever they perceive as strongest — which is always a dragon, or whoever is pretending to be one. Tunnelers speak in rapid-fire Draconic, constantly looking over their shoulders. They consider themselves the "chosen of dragons" and look down on goblins.

**Encounter Suggestions:**
- 6–8 Tunnelers defend a crude mineshaft, using caltrops and narrow passages to funnel the party (Easy–Medium for level 1)
- 2 Tunnelers are found scouting outside their territory, willing to trade information for shinies
- A collapsed tunnel has trapped several Tunnelers — do the party rescue them and earn favor, or leave them?
