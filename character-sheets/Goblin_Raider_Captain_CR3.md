# Goblin Raider Captain

*Small humanoid (goblinoid), neutral evil*

---

**Armor Class** 16 (chain shirt, shield)
**Hit Points** 55 (10d6 + 20)
**Speed** 30 ft.

---

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| 12 (+1) | 16 (+3) | 14 (+2) | 13 (+1) | 12 (+1) | 14 (+2) |

---

**Saving Throws** Dex +5, Cha +4
**Skills** Intimidation +4, Stealth +5, Perception +3
**Senses** darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 13
**Languages** Common, Goblin
**Challenge** 3 (700 XP)
**Proficiency Bonus** +2

---

## Traits

### Nimble Escape
The goblin can take the Disengage or Hide action as a bonus action on each of its turns.

### Rally the Warband (1/Day)
As a bonus action, the captain lets out a war cry. Each allied goblinoid within 30 feet that can hear the captain gains 5 temporary hit points and has advantage on its next attack roll.

### Cunning Ambusher
The captain and allied goblinoids within 30 feet gain a +3 bonus to initiative rolls while the captain is conscious.

---

## Actions

### Multiattack
The Raider Captain makes two melee attacks.

### Jagged Scimitar
*Melee Weapon Attack:* +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target.
*Hit:* 6 (1d6 + 3) slashing damage plus 3 (1d6) poison damage. The blade is coated in cave-spider venom.

### Barbed Net
*Ranged Weapon Attack:* +5 to hit, range 10/30 ft., one Large or smaller creature.
*Hit:* The target is restrained. A creature can use its action to make a DC 13 Strength check to free itself or another creature within reach. Dealing 5 slashing damage to the net (AC 10) frees the target without harming it.

---

## Reactions

### Redirect Attack
When a creature the captain can see targets it with an attack, the captain can choose an allied goblinoid within 5 feet. The two swap places, and the ally becomes the target of the attack instead.

---

## Tactics & Roleplaying Notes

**In Combat:** The Raider Captain opens with Rally the Warband, then targets the most dangerous-looking opponent with the Barbed Net before closing in with Jagged Scimitar. It positions itself behind its minions, using Redirect Attack to stay alive and Nimble Escape to reposition. It orders its skirmishers to focus fire on restrained targets.

**Personality:** Cruel and calculating — far smarter than it looks. The captain rules through a mix of fear and grudging respect. It wears trophies from past raids (a knight's signet ring, a merchant's hat) and speaks broken Common with mocking confidence.

**Encounter Suggestions:**
- 1 Raider Captain with 4–6 Goblin Skirmishers raiding a caravan (Medium for level 3–4)
- 2 Raider Captains competing for dominance, each with their own squad — the party can exploit the rivalry
- The captain leads a hostage negotiation, demanding ransom for captured travelers
