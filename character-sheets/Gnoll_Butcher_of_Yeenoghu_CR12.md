*Large fiend (gnoll, demon), chaotic evil*

## Traits

### Rampage
When the Butcher reduces a creature to 0 hit points with a melee attack on its turn, it can take a bonus action to move up to half its speed and make a bite attack.

### Legendary Resistance (3/Day)
If the Butcher fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.

### Yeenoghu's Chosen
The Butcher has been physically reshaped by Yeenoghu's power. It is Large, its attacks are magical, and it counts as both a fiend and a gnoll. It has advantage on saving throws against being banished. If the Butcher kills a humanoid, there is a 25% chance that a maw demon (or a hyena that transforms into a gnoll at the next dawn) spawns from the remains at the start of its next turn.

### Aura of the Charnel Pit (30 ft.)
The Butcher radiates an aura of hunger and rot. Each hostile creature that starts its turn within the aura must succeed on a DC 17 Constitution saving throw or take 7 (2d6) necrotic damage and be unable to regain hit points until the start of its next turn. Plant life within the aura withers and dies.

### Unending Hunger
The Butcher regains 10 hit points at the start of each of its turns if it has at least 1 hit point. If the Butcher takes radiant damage, this trait doesn't function at the start of its next turn.

## Actions

### Multiattack
The Butcher of Yeenoghu makes three attacks: two with the Flail of the Abyss and one with its Bite. It can replace one Flail attack with Rending Howl.

### Flail of the Abyss (Magical)
*Melee Weapon Attack:* +10 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target.
*Hit:* 15 (2d8 + 6) bludgeoning damage plus 9 (2d8) necrotic damage. If the target is a creature, it must succeed on a DC 18 Strength saving throw or be knocked prone.

### Bite
*Melee Weapon Attack:* +10 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target.
*Hit:* 11 (1d10 + 6) piercing damage plus 9 (2d8) necrotic damage. The Butcher regains hit points equal to the necrotic damage dealt.

### Rending Howl
The Butcher targets one creature it can see within 60 feet. The target must succeed on a DC 17 Wisdom saving throw or take 18 (4d8) psychic damage and be stunned until the end of its next turn. On a success, the target takes half damage and isn't stunned. A creature that succeeds on the save is immune to Rending Howl for 24 hours.

### Jaws of the Abyss (Recharge 5–6)
The Butcher slams its flail into the ground, and a 20-foot-radius circle of abyssal energy erupts at a point within 60 feet. Each creature in the area must make a DC 18 Dexterity saving throw, taking 36 (8d8) necrotic damage on a failure, or half on a success. Creatures killed by this damage rise as gnoll witherlings (use zombie statistics but with 30 ft. speed and the Rampage trait) at the start of the Butcher's next turn under its telepathic control.

---

## Bonus Actions

### Slaughter Command
The Butcher telepathically commands up to three allied gnolls or fiends it can see within 60 feet. Each target can use its reaction to move up to its speed and make one melee attack.

---

## Reactions

### Abyssal Backlash
When the Butcher takes damage from a spell, it can force the caster to make a DC 17 Constitution saving throw. On a failure, the caster takes 14 (4d6) necrotic damage and its concentration is broken. On a success, the caster takes half damage and maintains concentration.

---

## Legendary Actions

The Butcher can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action can be used at a time and only at the end of another creature's turn. The Butcher regains spent legendary actions at the start of its turn.

### Move
The Butcher moves up to half its speed. This movement doesn't provoke opportunity attacks.

### Feast (Costs 2 Actions)
The Butcher makes one Bite attack. If the attack hits, it regains additional hit points equal to the total damage dealt (both piercing and necrotic).

### Carrion Call (Costs 2 Actions)
The Butcher summons 1d4 hyenas or 1 gnoll witherling (zombie statistics with 30 ft. speed and Rampage) in unoccupied spaces within 30 feet. They act on initiative count 10.

### Apocalyptic Roar (Costs 3 Actions)
Each creature within 30 feet must succeed on a DC 17 Wisdom saving throw or be frightened of the Butcher until the end of its next turn. Each allied gnoll within 60 feet is healed for 10 hit points and can immediately move up to half its speed without provoking opportunity attacks.

---

## Lair Actions

*If encountered in the Flayed Hollow — the defiled remains of a conquered settlement, soaked in blood and consecrated to Yeenoghu:*

On initiative count 20 (losing initiative ties), the Butcher can take one lair action. It can't use the same effect two rounds in a row:

- **The Ground Hungers.** A 15-foot-radius area of ground within 60 feet becomes a swirling mass of gnashing spectral teeth. Each creature in the area must succeed on a DC 15 Dexterity saving throw or take 10 (3d6) piercing damage and be restrained until the end of its next turn.
- **Carrion Swarm.** A cloud of biting flies fills a 20-foot-radius sphere within 60 feet. The area is lightly obscured. Each creature that enters the area for the first time on a turn or starts its turn there takes 7 (2d6) necrotic damage. The swarm lasts until initiative count 20 of the next round.
- **Abyssal Rift.** A crack to the Abyss opens at a point within 60 feet. One shadow demon (or 2 dretches) emerges and acts on initiative count 15. The rift closes immediately. A maximum of 3 demons can be summoned this way per encounter.
- **Feast of the Fallen.** Each corpse within 60 feet is consumed by abyssal energy. The Butcher regains 5 hit points per corpse consumed. Consumed corpses cannot be revived by any spell short of *wish*.

## Tactics & Roleplaying Notes

**In Combat:** The Butcher is an engine of attrition. Its Unending Hunger and Bite sustain it through prolonged fights, while Aura of the Charnel Pit denies enemy healing. It opens with Jaws of the Abyss to deal massive AoE damage and raise the dead as witherlings, then wades into melee, prioritizing spellcasters with Rending Howl to shut down healing and crowd control. It uses Slaughter Command to coordinate its minion horde and Carrion Call to replenish losses. In its lair, it uses Feast of the Fallen to sustain itself on corpses and Abyssal Rift to escalate the encounter over time.

**Key Vulnerability:** Radiant damage shuts down Unending Hunger for a round. Clerics and paladins are the Butcher's priority targets for this reason — it will use Rending Howl and focus-fire to eliminate them first.

**Personality:** The Butcher is an abyssal horror wearing a gnoll's skin. It has intelligence enough for tactics and cruelty but no capacity for reason, mercy, or self-preservation. It speaks through telepathy in fragmented images of slaughter, transmitted directly into the minds of those within 60 feet — not as communication, but as psychological warfare. It laughs constantly, a low hyena cackle that never stops, even when wounded.

**Lore:** A Butcher of Yeenoghu is not merely a gnoll champion — it is an incursion event. When enough death and suffering have been offered in Yeenoghu's name, the demon lord reaches across the planes and reshapes his most devoted follower into a living gate between the Abyss and the Material Plane. The Butcher's presence warps the land: crops fail, animals flee or go mad, water turns brackish, and the dead do not rest. A Butcher left unchecked will transform an entire region into a howling wasteland within months.

**Encounter Suggestions:**
- The Butcher leads a final assault on a fortified city — the party must hold the walls or assassinate the Butcher while its army rampages (Deadly for level 10–12)
- The party tracks the Butcher to the Flayed Hollow, where it is performing a ritual to permanently open an Abyssal gate — disrupting the ritual is as important as defeating the creature
- An alliance of desperate factions (including the Goblin Warlord Ascendant) petitions the party to destroy the Butcher before it consumes everything
- The Butcher has killed a powerful NPC ally of the party, and their remains are being used in the Feast of the Fallen — the party must recover the body before it is consumed or resurrection becomes impossible
