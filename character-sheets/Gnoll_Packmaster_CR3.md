# Gnoll Packmaster

*Medium humanoid (gnoll), chaotic evil*

---

**Armor Class** 14 (hide armor, bone pauldrons)
**Hit Points** 52 (8d8 + 16)
**Speed** 30 ft.

---

|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| 16 (+3) | 14 (+2) | 14 (+2) | 8 (−1) | 12 (+1) | 12 (+1) |

---

**Saving Throws** Str +5, Con +4
**Skills** Animal Handling +3, Intimidation +3, Perception +3, Survival +3
**Senses** darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 13
**Languages** Gnoll, understands Abyssal
**Challenge** 3 (700 XP)
**Proficiency Bonus** +2

---

## Traits

### Rampage
When the gnoll reduces a creature to 0 hit points with a melee attack on its turn, the gnoll can take a bonus action to move up to half its speed and make a bite attack.

### Alpha of the Pack
Allied gnolls and hyenas within 30 feet of the Packmaster deal an extra 2 (1d4) damage with melee attacks while the Packmaster is conscious. When the Packmaster hits a creature with a melee attack, each allied hyena within 30 feet that can see the Packmaster can use its reaction to move up to half its speed toward the target.

### Blood Frenzy
The Packmaster has advantage on melee attack rolls against any creature that doesn't have all its hit points.

---

## Actions

### Multiattack
The Packmaster makes two attacks: one with its Flail of Teeth and one with its Bite.

### Flail of Teeth
*Melee Weapon Attack:* +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target.
*Hit:* 7 (1d8 + 3) bludgeoning damage plus 3 (1d6) piercing damage from embedded teeth and bone shards.

### Bite
*Melee Weapon Attack:* +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target.
*Hit:* 5 (1d4 + 3) piercing damage. If the target is a creature, it must succeed on a DC 13 Constitution saving throw or contract a disease. A diseased creature suffers disadvantage on Constitution saving throws and its hit point maximum is reduced by 3 (1d6) for each 24 hours that elapse. The disease lasts until cured by *lesser restoration* or similar magic.

### War Howl (Recharge 6)
The Packmaster unleashes a howl audible up to 300 feet away. Each allied gnoll and hyena within 60 feet that is frightened or charmed can immediately repeat the saving throw, ending the effect on a success. Additionally, each allied gnoll and hyena within 60 feet gains advantage on attack rolls until the start of the Packmaster's next turn.

---

## Reactions

### Punish the Weak
When an allied gnoll or hyena within 5 feet of the Packmaster is reduced to 0 hit points, the Packmaster can make one Flail of Teeth attack against the creature that dealt the killing blow, if that creature is within reach.

## Tactics & Roleplaying Notes

**In Combat:** The Packmaster leads from the front, always surrounded by its hyena pack. It opens with War Howl to empower its allies, then charges the most wounded enemy to exploit Blood Frenzy. Alpha of the Pack ensures that its hyenas swarm whatever it attacks, turning single targets into a feeding frenzy. It fights until dead — Packmasters do not flee and will kill retreating gnolls who try.

**Companion Creatures:** The Packmaster is typically accompanied by 2–4 hyenas (CR 0) and 2–4 Gnoll Scavengers. The hyenas act as a pack, surrounding enemies and exploiting the extra damage from Alpha of the Pack.

**Personality:** More cunning than a Scavenger but still brutish. The Packmaster communicates through violence, dominance displays, and guttural commands. It is the strongest gnoll in its band and maintains control by maiming challengers. It has a rudimentary cunning — it understands ambush tactics, tracks prey for days, and knows to attack settlements at night.

**Encounter Suggestions:**
- 1 Packmaster with 4 Scavengers and 3 hyenas attacks a farmstead at night (Medium for level 3–4)
- The party discovers the Packmaster's trail — scattered bones, claw marks on trees, distant howling — and must decide whether to hunt or be hunted
- The Packmaster has captured prisoners and is herding them toward a larger gnoll warband; the party intercepts en route
