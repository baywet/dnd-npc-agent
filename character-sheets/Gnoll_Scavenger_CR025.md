## Traits

### Rampage
When the gnoll reduces a creature to 0 hit points with a melee attack on its turn, the gnoll can take a bonus action to move up to half its speed and make a bite attack.

### Carrion Sense
The gnoll has advantage on Wisdom (Perception) and Wisdom (Survival) checks that rely on smell. It can detect the scent of blood or decay within 1 mile.

### Hunger-Driven
The gnoll has advantage on melee attack rolls against creatures that are bloodied (at or below half hit points).

## Tactics & Roleplaying Notes

**In Combat:** Gnoll Scavengers are driven by hunger, not strategy. They charge the weakest-looking target, trying to down it quickly so Rampage can chain into another attack. Hunger-Driven makes them increasingly dangerous as a fight drags on and enemies take wounds. They are relentless — they do not flee from food. They only retreat from fire or obviously overwhelming force.

**Personality:** Scavengers are barely sapient — more feral animal than person. They communicate in barks, cackles, and crude Gnoll. They fight over scraps, follow the strongest gnoll without question, and attack anything edible. They have no concept of negotiation or mercy.

**Encounter Suggestions:**
- 4–6 Scavengers descend on the aftermath of a battle, initially ignoring the party in favor of corpses — but turning hostile if challenged (Easy–Medium for level 1)
- A pack of Scavengers has been trailing the party for days, waiting for someone to get injured
- 2 Scavengers are found gnawing on a destroyed merchant cart — investigation leads to a larger gnoll warband nearby
